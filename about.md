<h2>Hello!</h2>

<p>
I am Sophia, a psychology graduate with a Master's degree in Behaviour Change Psychology.
I am passionate about understanding why people think, feel, and behave the way they do,
and how psychological research can help us create meaningful change.
</p>

<p>
Through my studies, I have developed a particular interest in behaviour change,
mental health, and the connection between psychological science and everyday life.
I enjoy exploring how theories and research findings can be translated into
practical insights that people can apply in their own lives.
</p>

</div>


<div class="card">

<h2>My Background</h2>

<p>
I completed my Bachelor's degree in Psychology at Radboud University, followed by a
Master's degree in Behaviour Change Psychology. During my studies, I gained experience
with psychological research methods, data analysis, and designing interventions aimed
at understanding and changing behaviour.
</p>

<p>
My academic interests include health psychology, sustainable behaviour, motivation,
and the psychological factors that influence the choices we make every day.
</p>

</div>


<div class="card">

<h2>Why This Website?</h2>

<p>
Psychology research is fascinating, but scientific knowledge can sometimes feel
difficult to access. Through this blog, I want to explore psychological topics in a
way that is scientifically grounded while remaining understandable and relevant
to everyday life.
</p>

<p>
My goal is to bridge the gap between academic research and the people who are
curious about understanding themselves and others better.
</p>
